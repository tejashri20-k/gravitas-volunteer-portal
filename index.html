About Gravitas 2026

Gravitas is the annual techno-management festival conducted by VIT. Every year, hundreds of student volunteers step up to run events, welcome guests and keep the festival moving. This portal collects volunteer registrations for the organizing committee.

Technical events

Support hackathons, workshops and competitions from setup to the final round.

Management events

Help run case competitions, guest talks and panel discussions.

On-ground support

Work at registration desks, hospitality, logistics and publicity.
